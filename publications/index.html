Publications

My research combines nuclear astrophysics, uncertainty-aware nuclear theory, and experimental collaborations in nuclear structure and decay spectroscopy. This page highlights current themes, recent work, and a broader collaborative publication record.

Current themes

  • r-process nucleosynthesis, β-decay and fission
  • Machine learning and surrogate models for nuclear astrophysics
  • Uncertainty quantification through Bayesian statistics
  • Experimental nuclear structure and decay spectroscopy collaborations

Recent work
